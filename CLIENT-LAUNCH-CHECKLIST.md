# LittleNest — client launch checklist

## Business information
- [ ] Correct business name, logo, phone, email and social links
- [ ] Seller-approved product names, package contents and descriptions
- [ ] Approved product price and any quantity discounts
- [ ] Accurate delivery areas, fees and delivery estimates
- [ ] Accurate payment, cancellation, returns and refund policies

## Content and trust
- [ ] All product images are owned or licensed for use
- [ ] All claims can be substantiated
- [ ] Reviews are genuine and used with permission
- [ ] No fake countdowns, false scarcity or invented guarantees
- [ ] Privacy notice and contact details are visible

## Form and operations
- [ ] Client controls the Formspree account where possible
- [ ] Correct endpoint is in `config.js`
- [ ] Correct notification email is verified in Formspree
- [ ] Form submission tested on the deployed URL
- [ ] Order total matches the approved pricing rules
- [ ] Delivery fees are clearly described and not misrepresented as included
- [ ] Client knows a form submission is a request, not payment or order confirmation

## Technical handover
- [ ] Mobile, tablet and desktop layouts checked
- [ ] Navigation, links and form validation checked
- [ ] Page title and meta description updated
- [ ] Domain connected and HTTPS active
- [ ] Source code and hosting access handed over securely
- [ ] Maintenance/support scope and fees agreed
